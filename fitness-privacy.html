Privacy & account deletion

Updated 22 October 2026. EZPZ Fitness is currently a private test operated by Kasper Reimers. Contact: reimerskasper@gmail.com.

Your device records

You can use the app without an account. Your profile, measurements, workouts, food records, sleep, steps, plans, preferences and any added photos are stored in this browser on this device. Available records depend on what you enter.

Someone with access to this browser can read retained records, including after sign-out. Clearing browser storage can remove them. Download a backup before changing devices.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud backup

Google supplies your account identity and email to sign you in. Supabase manages authentication and holds your cloud backup. Sign-in alone does not upload fitness records.

Save device progress to cloud uploads a snapshot of your app records. Later edits stay on the device until you save again. A later save replaces the previous cloud snapshot. Restore cloud progress replaces the records in the browser you are using, after confirmation and a requested safety download.

The backup is associated with your signed-in account. Google sign-in does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or wearable records. Wearable connections require separate permission. Google and Supabase process authentication and technical information under their own policies.

Optional wearable connections

The Android test app can import WHOOP records through Health Connect after you grant read permissions. Imports happen when you choose Sync now and are saved on your device. The currently supported measurements are steps, sleep duration, optional deep and REM sleep, and calories burned. Optional nutrition permission also lets EZPZ read the last seven days of shared food calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat from Health Connect apps such as Cronometer. Nutrition is imported when you connect, refresh, or open/return to EZPZ with a connected nutrition source. It is stored by source and date and included in optional backups. One external nutrition source is selected at a time; EZPZ food logs are added to that source’s totals. Daily imports cannot identify duplicate meals automatically. Log each meal in one app or mark an EZPZ meal as already logged in the connected app to exclude it from combined totals. Recipe photos are not imported. Missing measurements are not estimated. Manual records stay saved.

A direct WHOOP connection is available with separate authorization. If you authorize it, Supabase stores encrypted WHOOP access and refresh tokens linked to your EZPZ account so the server can request permitted data. Tokens are not sent to the app. This encryption is server managed; it is not end-to-end encryption against the operator. Authorization requests expire after ten minutes. After you authorize WHOOP, EZPZ refreshes permitted measurements and completed workouts when you open or return to the app, while online. Opening the app does not upload your device records to cloud backup. You may disconnect WHOOP to stop these refreshes. The server reads the selected measurements and returns them to your device; it does not keep a separate fitness-data copy outside an optional cloud backup.

You can revoke access in WHOOP or Health Connect. To delete stored connection credentials, delete your EZPZ cloud account or contact the operator. Account deletion removes the active WHOOP credential and pending authorization records; local measurements and downloaded backups remain until you delete those copies. Imported measurements are included in a cloud backup only when you choose Save.

Technical information

GitHub Pages hosts the app. Hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request details and service logs to operate their services. The browser stores a sign-in session and cached app files for offline use. Refer to the providers for their retention practices.

No advertising or analytics tracker is added by the app. Cloud backups are not end-to-end encrypted by the app; the service operator and infrastructure providers can have administrative access. Do not put information in a backup that you do not want stored with the service.

Delete your data

On this device: open More → Back up my data → Delete records from this device. This removes app fitness records from that browser; it does not delete the cloud account or backup. Delete downloaded backup files separately. Other devices keep their own copies.

Cloud account and backup: during this private test, email reimerskasper@gmail.com from your signed-in email and request deletion. We will verify account ownership before removing the account and its active cloud backup. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. An in-app deletion control is being prepared and is not yet activated.

Cloud records stay until replaced or deleted; there is currently no automatic expiry. Provider logs and infrastructure backups follow the providers’ retention processes and may not disappear immediately. Deletion does not remove files you downloaded or copies retained on devices. Deleting an EZPZ account does not delete your Google account. Signing in again later can create a new, empty EZPZ account.

This private test is intended for adults aged 18 or older. For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, use the contact email above.
