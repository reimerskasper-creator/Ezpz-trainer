# Meal sections
User requested High protein, High carb, High fat, and Balanced sections, preserving the established meal photography.

The first 49 recipes, indices, nutrition references and images remain unchanged. Eighteen appended recipes (six per focus) adapt gram weights of existing dishes, preserving the ingredient set and reusing the matching dish photograph. These are portion variants, not newly photographed dishes. Photos are serving illustrations; the listed grams control calories, logging, shopping and planning.

High protein variants provide at least 35% of macro-derived energy from protein; high carb at least 55% from carbohydrate; high fat at least 40% from fat. These are app sorting conventions, not regulatory nutrient claims. Energy shares use 4/4/9 for consistency; displayed calories use the existing per-100g ingredient table.

Four compact two-column photo tiles select a section. Balanced opens initially; the section stays selected while viewing a recipe. Existing breakfast/lunch/dinner and ingredient search combine with it, including counts and empty feedback. All 67 recipes are also available through the existing planner photo picker.

No storage schema changes. Existing recipe indices and stored plans retain their meaning. Unit checks protect original data and unchanged food functions except the two deliberately updated browsing functions (eat/filterRecipes). New checks cover focus thresholds, same-dish images, scaled weights, filters and Chromium layouts.
